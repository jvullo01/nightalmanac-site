When does the Moon clear your horizon tonight?

Night Almanac: Moon & Planets is an iPhone almanac for the night sky. It gives moonrise, the Moon's highest point and moonset for the place you choose, plus the planets that are up after dark, all calculated on the phone.

What it does

Moon times to the minute

Tested against 269 U.S. Naval Observatory moonrises and moonsets in six cities: the largest difference is 0.54 minutes. "Tonight" runs noon to noon, and clock-change days get their real length.

The planets tonight

Mercury to Neptune: rise, highest point, set, direction and brightness. Checked against NASA JPL Horizons; within about a minute, except when a planet only grazes the horizon.

Phase calendar

A month of Moon phases at a glance, with a list layout for large text sizes.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets that show the same night as the app.

Reminders

Optional alerts for full Moon rising, new Moon, supermoons and eclipses, scheduled on the phone as local notifications.

34,188 places, offline

A built-in list of 34,188 cities with their time zones. Search works without a connection.

Private by design

No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics. The app contains no networking code and collects no data. Read the privacy policy.

Sources and credits

  • Moon and Sun times are compared in tests with published data from the U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO).
  • Planet times are compared in tests with NASA JPL Horizons ephemerides.
  • Calculations follow Jean Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms, and JPL's approximate planetary elements.
  • City list: GeoNames, licensed CC BY 4.0.
