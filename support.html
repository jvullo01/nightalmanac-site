Questions and answers

How do I choose a place?
Tap the place name on Tonight and search the built-in list of 34,188 cities. It works offline. You can also allow approximate location and the app picks the nearest listed city.
My town isn't listed.
Choose the nearest listed city. Rise and set times change gradually with distance, so a nearby city is usually very close.
How are time zones and daylight saving handled?
Each place carries its own time zone, and times are shown on that place's local clock. On clock-change days the day really is 23 or 25 hours long (23½ or 24½ at Lord Howe Island), and the app accounts for that.
Why might a planet time differ by a minute from another source?
Near the horizon, small differences in assumed refraction and elevation shift rise and set times. When a planet barely skims the horizon, it moves almost sideways, so tiny differences can change the time more than usual. Outside those grazing passes, the app's planet times are within about a minute of NASA JPL Horizons.
Why does "Tonight" show a time marked "earlier"?
Tonight runs from noon to the next noon. Events in that window that have already passed are labelled "earlier".
I'm not getting reminders.
Check that the reminder is on in the app and that notifications for Night Almanac are allowed in iPhone Settings › Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on the phone, so no connection is needed.
How do I reach support?
Email jvappsupport+nightalmanac@proton.me. Including your place and the date helps with time questions.
