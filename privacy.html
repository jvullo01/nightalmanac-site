Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Night Almanac collects no data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

The short version

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs.
  • No tracking. The app's privacy manifest declares no tracking, no tracking domains and no collected data types.
  • The app has no networking code of its own, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else. The only exception is an optional tip, which is handled entirely by Apple (see below).

Location

Using your location is optional; you can pick a place from the list instead. If you allow it, the app asks for permission only while in use, at reduced (approximate) accuracy. It rounds the coordinates and matches them to the nearest city in its built-in list so it can work out your sky. Your location is used on the device only and is never sent anywhere.

Reminders

Reminders are optional. If you turn one on, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them as local notifications on your iPhone. No push service or server is involved.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • Your chosen place and recent places (city name, coordinates, time zone), kept in a shared app container so the widgets can show the same place.
  • Your reminder choices.

This data stays on the device. Deleting the app removes it. The privacy manifest lists one required-reason API, UserDefaults, used only for these settings.

Optional tips

Night Almanac is free. It may offer an optional tip: a consumable in-app purchase through Apple that unlocks nothing. If you choose to tip, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment-card details, your name or any other personal information about who tipped.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
